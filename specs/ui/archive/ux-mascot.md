# ux-mascot: the sumo reacts when poked and does the work himself

New requirements:
- `UI-MASCOT-POKE` (manual): the header sumo is a button; jump height tracks girth, strain when maxed, shiko at 3 clicks, dizzy at 6+, keyboard activation, reduced-motion blink, deflate after space is freed, idle fidgets only while focused.
- `UI-MASCOT-JOBS` (manual): an approved job sends the sumo down a ladder to its card to act the job out on a parcel (one move per action, plus fail, cancel, and decline reactions), then back up, pleased; off-screen, long-job, and repeat fallbacks; reduced motion; never delays the job or blocks input.

Modified: none (`UI-MASCOT-FULLNESS` still holds). Removed: none.
