"Wait. What is this?"

Open anything in Your call and he tells you what it is and why he thinks so. He checks the facts first, then explains. He only searches the web if he still isn't sure.

House rules

  1. Nothing happens until you press. He suggests. You decide.
  2. Delete means holding the button. No slips from one stray click.
  3. Delete is for real. It skips the Recycle Bin, because the bin frees nothing until you empty it.
  4. Your word wins. His guesses never outrank you. What you said to keep stays put.
  5. Keep it, and he stops asking. Kept things stop showing up, with one link to bring them back.
  6. He stays out of the way. One window. He keeps a list of your drive and updates it quietly in the background.

Your own provider

OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Together, Cerebras, or anything that speaks the OpenAI format.

Online choices send your questions, and the file and folder names he looks up, to that provider. The on-this-PC brain sends nothing.
