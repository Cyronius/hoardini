How he decides

The order on the bill is the order he trusts things in.

  1. Evidence firstA catalog of known locations, file types, the list of programs you installed, and checks for files that are open right now.
  2. Then the AI explainsIt reads the evidence and puts it in plain words: what the thing is, and why he thinks so.
  3. The web lastOnly when the first two can’t tell.

House rules

Nothing happens until you press. He suggests; you decide. Delete needs the button held. No slips. Delete is permanent. It skips the Recycle Bin, because the bin frees nothing until it is emptied. Your word wins. His guesses never outrank you. Only what you said to keep is held back. Kept means kept. Things you keep stop showing up, with one link to bring them back. Some paths stay off limits. Rules in code can hold any path back, whatever anyone says. He stays out of the way. One window. He keeps a list of the whole drive and updates it quietly, in a low-priority process.
