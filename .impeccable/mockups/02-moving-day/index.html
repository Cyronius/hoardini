One short screen. Three boxes.

No tree of folders to dig through. Hoardini looks at the whole drive and hands you three boxes, biggest win first. Press the box. The space comes back.

He gets bigger as your drive fills up. Watch what happens when you empty the boxes.

“What is this?” Answered on a packing slip.

Open anything in the Ask me box and he tells you what it is, in plain words, and why he thinks so.

He checks the evidence first: where it lives, what kind of files are in it, which programs you have installed, and whether anything has it open. Then the AI explains. The web comes last.

Handle with care. He does.

  • Nothing moves till you press

    He sorts and explains. Nothing is deleted, shrunk or moved until you press the button.

  • Hold to delete

    Delete needs the button held down, so a slip of the mouse can't empty a box.

  • No Recycle Bin

    Delete is permanent. The bin frees nothing until it's emptied, so he skips it on purpose.

  • Your word wins

    His guesses never outrank you. What you keep stops showing up, with one link to bring it back.

One window. No tray icon, no pop-ups. He keeps a list of the whole drive and updates it quietly in the background.
